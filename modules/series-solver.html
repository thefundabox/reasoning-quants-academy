The 8 Patterns That Cover 95% of Questions

Always test in this order: differences → ratios → squares/cubes → alternate terms.

1. Arithmetic (AP)
5, 9, 13, 17, 21

Constant difference (+4). The first thing to check, always.

2. Geometric (GP)
3, 6, 12, 24, 48

Constant ratio (×2). Differences grow fast — try dividing.

3. Squares & Cubes
4, 9, 16, 25, 36

n² or n³, sometimes ± a constant (n²+1: 2, 5, 10, 17…).

4. Growing differences
2, 4, 8, 14, 22, 32

Differences themselves form a series (+2, +4, +6, +8, +10).

5. Multiply & adjust
3, 7, 15, 31, 63

×2+1 each time. Variants: ×2−1, ×3−2…

6. Sum of previous
2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21

Fibonacci family: each term = sum of the two before it.

7. Alternating twin series
1, 10, 3, 8, 5, 6, 7

Two series interleaved: odd positions +2, even positions −2.

8. Letter jumps
A, D, H, M, S

Jumps of +3, +4, +5 positions. Convert letters to numbers (A=1)!
