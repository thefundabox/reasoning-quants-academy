The 5-Symbol Language

Every blood-relation puzzle — no matter how twisted — is built from only five marks. Master these and you can draw anything.

Square = Malefather, son, brother, uncle…
Circle = Femalemother, daughter, sister, aunt…
Double line = Marriagehusband ═ wife
Vertical line = Parent → Childone generation down
Single line = Siblingsbrothers / sisters, same level

The Generation Ladder

"You" are always Level 0 — every answer is just a climb up or down this ladder.

+2Grandfather · Grandmother · Great-uncle
+1Father · Mother · Uncle · Aunt
0You · Brother · Sister · Cousin · Wife
−1Son · Daughter · Nephew · Niece
−2Grandson · Granddaughter

The 3-Step Solving Method

Use this exact sequence on every question — statement type, coded type, or "pointing at a photo" type.

1

Anchor. Find the fixed person — usually the speaker ("I", "he", the photo). Place them at Level 0. Break the sentence at every comma or "of".

2

Draw backwards. "Pointing to a photo, he said, 'She is the daughter of my grandfather's only son'" → start from grandfather's only son and walk toward the photo, one symbol at a time. Never solve a chain left-to-right in your head.

3

Count the ladder gap. Once the tree is drawn, read the level difference and gender shape off the diagram. The diagram is the answer — no memory needed.
